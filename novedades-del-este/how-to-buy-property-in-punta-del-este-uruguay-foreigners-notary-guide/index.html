Buying pre-construction or new-build property in Punta del Este · Maldonado from abroad (United States, Europe, Argentina, or Brazil) does not require local citizenship or traveling for the initial reservation. In Uruguay, property titles are denominated in US Dollars (USD), and every transaction is audited by a public notary (Escribano Público).

1. Exact Documentation Required for Non-Resident Buyers (Individuals & Foreign LLCs)

Unlike generic portals that only send a marketing brochure, ZAGUÁN prepares your complete compliance folder alongside our allied notary before you wire a reservation deposit:

  • Valid ID: Copy of your Passport or national ID (DNI/Cédula), plus a signed Personal KYC Form (and power of attorney if signing remotely through a representative).
  • Proof of Income for Off-Plan Installments: When buying pre-construction in installments (such as Betania IV from USD 79,800, Nostrum Roosevelt from USD 107,419, More Atlántico from USD 120,000, EVE Tower from USD 150,000, or Golden Rise up to 150 installments), you only need your last 3 payslips (>6 months tenure) or a CPA-certified annual income statement, plus proof of origin of funds if proceeds come from a prior property sale.
  • Foreign Legal Entities (LLCs / Corporations): Legalized and translated articles of incorporation, certificate of good standing issued within 45 days, and identification of shareholders owning 15% or more.

2. Law 18.795 (10-Year Tax Exemption) & Uruguay Fiscal Residency (Decree 163/020)

  • Law 18.795 (Promoted Housing — Nostrum Roosevelt & More Atlántico): Exempts the buyer from the 2% property transfer tax (ITP) at closing, exempts Wealth Tax (Impuesto al Patrimonio) for 10 years, and exempts 100% of rental income tax (IRPF/IRAE) for 10 years.
  • Fiscal Residency in Uruguay (Decree 163/020): Foreigners investing over 3,500,000 UI in Uruguayan real estate with at least 60 days of physical presence per calendar year qualify for fiscal residency as soon as the notarized and registered Purchase Commitment (Compromiso de Compraventa) is signed—even if the property is financed in installments.
